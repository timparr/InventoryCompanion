# Active implementation plans

Keep provisional and approved implementation plans here as
`<Feature>ImplementationPlan.md`, linked to their specifications in
[FeatureSpecs](../FeatureSpecs).

Create plans from the [implementation-plan template](../../Processes/Templates/ImplementationPlan.md)
and follow the [feature development process](../../Processes/FeatureDevelopment.md).
Being stored here does not mean a plan is approved or coding is authorized.

After explicit feature acceptance, move the completed plan to its feature folder
under [Archive](../Archive), mark it historical, and update all inbound and
relative links. Keep the delivered authoritative specification in FeatureSpecs.
