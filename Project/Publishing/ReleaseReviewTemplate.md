## Review confirmations

- [ ] ESOUI release guidance, upload rules, and linked prohibited-functionality guidance

- [ ] Addon globals, saved-variable names and scope, UI names, and event filters

## Notes and references

Record relevant version checks, in-game test results, exact package-content verification, and any ESOUI staff clarification here. The human checker must verify author-only claims such as distribution rights, credits, and listing details. Do not mark the review approved while a required compliance question or test remains unresolved.
